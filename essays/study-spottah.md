# Study Spottah

Problem: Finding study spots on campus outside of the library can be hard, especially if you are looking for a specific vibe or certain amenities. 
Solution: This application will allow UHM students to ground source a list of “hidden gem” study spots and classify them by amenities and other descriptive labels. Users will be able to view and filter the table of different study spots and they could potentially propose new study spots or leave comments about the quality of the study. 

## Approach:
The main idea is to provide a list of study spots that are tagged with different descriptors to help students find new places to study on campus, which will be further known as Study Spotter. We’d have a solid amount of static data that we would have to collect and populate first for each available spot, such as:
- Location (Building, Floor, Room, Name)
- AC or not 
- Food allowed or not
- Chargers or not
- Windows or not
- Group study okay?
There will also be functionality to allow users to input additional data, such as:
- Loudness levels
- Traffic, busy or not?
- Available seating?
- Microwaves? 
- Quiet level [scale to 1-5]

## Mockup Page Ideas:
- Homepage with a list of all the study spots
- Page for each study spot with the above data points
- Subsection for users to report to website how crowded the spot is/loud ppl/traffic, etc.
- Users are able to submit a proposal for new study spots
- Study spots are required to have the following information: room, building, floor 
- Maybe have a drawing/diagram? 
- Labels: AC, Wall outlets, Foot traffic during passing, has microwave, can eat/drink, is busy 
- Use ArcGIS layer from UHM campus map

## Use Case Ideas:
- Need to find a nearby place to set up and study
- Add a study spot for admins to review
- View Study Spot Details
- Report How Busy a Study Spot is
- Report current noise level at a Study Spot at a scale of 1-5
- Leave review and feedback
- Map of study spots
